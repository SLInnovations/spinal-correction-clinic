ABC™ Chiropractic

Chiropractic care focused on structure.

Advanced BioStructural Correction™ is the specific chiropractic adjusting technique used at Spinal Correction Clinic.

Not all chiropractic techniques are the same.

ABC™ is a manual, protocol-based approach used to assess and address aspects of body structure and mechanics. At Spinal Correction Clinic it forms the foundation of chiropractic treatment rather than being presented as a separate service.

What happens during a visit?

Care begins with history and assessment. Findings are considered according to the ABC™ protocol, and manual corrections are performed when clinically appropriate.

Why use a specific technique?

A defined technique creates consistency in how the clinician assesses and delivers care. Dr. Mayall uses ABC™ as his primary chiropractic approach based on his training and clinical experience.
