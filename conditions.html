Conditions

Start with what’s bothering you.

Back Pain

Assessment considers the painful region together with relevant history, mobility and mechanics.

Neck Pain

Neck stiffness, restricted movement and mechanical neck complaints are assessed in context.

Headaches

Some headache presentations may have musculoskeletal contributors; assessment helps determine whether manual care is appropriate.

Shoulder & Arm

Local tissues and broader movement factors may both be relevant.

Hip & Knee

Assessment can include local and whole-body mechanics.

Sports & Activity Injuries

Recent injuries and older problems that have not fully resolved can be assessed.

Posture & Movement

Assessment focuses on clinically relevant musculoskeletal findings rather than appearance alone.

Persistent & Recurring Problems

If the same problem keeps returning, the clinic takes a broader history and examines other relevant mechanical or fascial findings.
