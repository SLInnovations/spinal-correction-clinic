Our Approach

Structure. Fascia. Function.

A broader way of looking at musculoskeletal complaints.

Start with the person, not just the symptom.

Pain is important information, but it is only one part of an assessment. Your current complaint is considered alongside relevant history, posture, movement, joint mechanics and soft-tissue findings.

Previous injuries, fractures, accidents, falls and surgeries may be relevant because the body adapts to what has happened before. That does not mean an old injury is automatically the cause of a new symptom.
